01
AIR COOLING
Traditional Forced-Air Method

Air cooling is the original ASIC cooling method — industrial fans push ambient air across the hashboards at high velocity, expelling heat from the rear exhaust. Every Antminer ships with this system by default.

The S21 air-cooled model pushes 200 TH/s at 17.5 J/TH with dual high-speed fans running at 75 dB. In Oman's 45–50°C summers, air-cooled miners require supplementary ambient cooling infrastructure to maintain stable chip temperatures.

✓ ADVANTAGES
Lowest initial investment
Easy installation & servicing
No special infrastructure needed
Wide hardware compatibility
Simple troubleshooting
✗ LIMITATIONS
Very loud (75–85 dB)
High ambient temperature dependency
No overclocking headroom
Shorter hardware lifespan
Poor fit for Oman summers
OTI RECOMMENDATION Best for small pilots and budget-conscious deployments. Pair with our air-conditioning infrastructure for stable Oman operations.
02
WATER / HYDRO COOLING
Closed-Loop Liquid Cooling — OTI Primary Method

Water cooling uses a closed-loop system where coolant fluid is pumped directly through the mining hardware via pipes and cold plates attached to the hashboards. Heat is transferred to an external heat exchanger rather than exhausted as hot air.

The Antminer S21 Hydro runs at 335 TH/s with 16 J/TH — our flagship at Muscat. The white looped pipes visible in our rack photos are the coolant input/output lines feeding each unit. At 840 L/min total flow across our facility, chip temperatures stay below 40°C even in peak Oman summer heat.

✓ ADVANTAGES
Industry-best efficiency (16 J/TH)
Operates in 50°C+ ambient temps
Dramatically reduced noise (40–50 dB)
+10–20% overclock headroom
Extended hardware lifespan 4–6 yrs
Scalable to any rack size
✗ LIMITATIONS
Higher upfront infrastructure cost
Requires piping & heat exchangers
Coolant maintenance required
Leak risk if poorly installed
⭐ OTI PRIMARY DEPLOYMENT This is what powers our 320-unit S21 Hydro fleet in Muscat. Perfect balance of cost, performance, and scalability for industrial operations in the GCC region.
03
IMMERSION OIL COOLING
Full Submersion Dielectric Fluid Method

Immersion cooling submerges entire ASIC units — circuit boards, chips, and all — into a bath of non-conductive dielectric fluid (mineral oil or engineered synthetic fluid). The fluid directly absorbs chip-level heat, providing the most efficient thermal transfer possible.

Unlike air or hydro cooling, there are no fans whatsoever. The result is near-complete silence, chip temperatures of 20–35°C, and the ability to overclock standard ASIC hardware by 30–40% above factory spec — turning a 200 TH/s unit into a 260–280 TH/s unit with no hardware changes.

✓ ADVANTAGES
30–40% hashrate boost via OC
Near-zero noise (<35 dB)
5–8 year hardware lifespan
Best J/TH efficiency achievable
No fan failure risk
Ambient-independent operation
✗ LIMITATIONS
Highest upfront capital cost
Dielectric fluid is expensive
Complex setup & commissioning
Harder to hot-swap hardware
Requires specialized tanks
OTI RECOMMENDATION Maximum ROI per watt. Ideal for institutional-scale deployments where capital is available and long-term efficiency is the priority. Ask us about immersion setup at your facility.
